Adds chart discovery and atomic title and series edits, available by default. Use `charts` and `editCharts`, or the agents tools `xlsx_list_charts`, `xlsx_read_chart_text` and `xlsx_edit_charts`, to replace or remove titles, rename series and change category or value references on unshared chart parts.
