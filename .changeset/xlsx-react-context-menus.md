Adds context menus for cells and sheet tabs (right-click, Menu key or Shift+F10) and the commands behind them: `cut`, `copy`, `paste`, `pastePlainText`, `clearContents`, `insertRows`, `insertColumns`, `deleteRows`, `deleteColumns`, `insertSheet`, `deleteSheet`, `renameSheet` and `moveSheet`.
