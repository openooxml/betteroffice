Inline charts and text boxes now sit side by side on a line like inline pictures, as in Word, instead of each taking a block of its own, so landscape pages with two charts no longer spill onto a second page. Floating charts now keep their position and text wrapping instead of being stacked in the text, so charts placed over each other or in front of text no longer push the following pages down.
