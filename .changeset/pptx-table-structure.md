Adds `insertTableRow`, `deleteTableRow`, `insertTableColumn` and `deleteTableColumn`, with matching `pptx_edit` operations available by default. Edits preserve formatting, refresh refs, and support undo and collaboration; merged tables, deleting the last row or column, and concurrent structure conflicts are refused.
