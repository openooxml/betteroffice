Show where printer-paper boundaries fall across a page. The display list gains `printWidth` and `printHeight` (contract version 6), taken from the page's `PaperKind`, print orientation and margins at the page's drawing scale, and a page-break toggle on the ribbon draws the boundaries over the page. A page whose file names no printer paper reports its own extent, one sheet.
