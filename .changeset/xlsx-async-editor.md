Enables `collaboration` and remote presence with `XlsxEditor asyncApi`, opt-in, through `XlsxAsyncEditorApi.collaboration` and `createAsyncCollaborationReplica`. Adds experimental `XlsxAsyncEditorApi.recoverySave`, which keeps accepted edits, undo and redo across worker failures and leaves the editor read-only.
