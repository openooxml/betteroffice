The calculation engine now evaluates Excel's engineering functions: base conversions (BIN2DEC through OCT2HEX), the BIT functions, DELTA, GESTEP, COMPLEX, every IM complex-number function and the four Bessel functions. Complex results are written as Excel writes them.
