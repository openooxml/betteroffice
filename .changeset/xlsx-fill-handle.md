Selected ranges can show an opt-in fill handle (`fillHandle` prop); dragging it copies the block down or right, adjusting relative formulas, as one undo step. Fills refuse arrays, merges, tables, chart anchors, hyperlinks, partially covering conditional formats, and sheets with comments or data validations.
